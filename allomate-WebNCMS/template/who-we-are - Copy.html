A brief history lesson

Khan Law was founded by the inimitable Shahid M. Khan in 2000 with a commitment to provide high quality, cost-effective, and personalized client services. Now acting as the firm's Managing Director, Shahid is joined by the next generation of Khan lawyers - his daughter Anam and son Faraz. The family-owned firm provides legal services across a number of areas including real estate, family law, and wills + estates.

The importance of family is woven throughout their practice; illustrated by working together to secure optimal results for clients, and understanding the sensitivity of clients' cases - whether it be purchasing a new home or an emotionally arduous family matter. Rest assured, Khan Law is on your side.
