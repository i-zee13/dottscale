Real estate

The purchase and sale of a home can be a stressful process if you do not have the right team. At Khan Law, we buy and sell hundreds of properties each year for satisfied clients. We accommodate your needs and provide timely updates to give you peace of mind. We keep the process simple for you, and provide utmost transparency in our fees and disbursements.

Wills + estates

Whether you need a simple will or estate litigation, Khan Law can help you. No matter the size of your estate, having a will is essential for you and your loved ones. We take the time to understand your wishes and advise of potential road blocks and how to navigate them so that your will is carried out.

Immigration

At Khan Law, our team of experienced estate litigation lawyers can provide you with the necessary advice and guidance to help you plan ahead in your personal affairs or navigate tough and emotional legal situations.

Business litigation

With businesses - both domestic and global - becoming more complex, Khan Law provides you with tailored legal advice for your specific needs. It is more beneficial and cost effective to seek legal opinion before embarking on your venture than to do so retrospectively. Whether you are ready to start a new business, or are looking for corporate restructuring, we will support and guide you through any legal challenges with minimal impact to you and your business.

Personal injury

Your wellbeing and interests are paramount to us. We aim to deliver maximum entitlements to you prescribed under the law for your losses. Khan Law mitigates work injuries, automobile and other accidents, defective products, medical errors, slip and fall accidents, and more. We put you first, and in most cases, we work with you on a No-Win-No-Fee basis.

Criminal

In any criminal proceeding you must have complete confidence in your defence. It is essential to know that you have the right team of lawyers who are passionate in their field and committed to your case. When you are in trouble with the law, we will take every step to enforce your rights, ensuring they are protected and safeguarded.
